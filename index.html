How it works

1

Start a chat

Click "Start a new chat". You instantly get two things: an encrypted invite link and a passphrase.

2

Share them separately

Send the link via WhatsApp, email, or any app. Send the passphrase a different way: a phone call, SMS, or say it in person. Keeping them apart means nobody can intercept both.

3

They open the link & reply

The other person opens your link, types the passphrase, and gets a reply link that is automatically copied. They send it back to you.

4

Paste & you're connected

Paste their reply link and you're instantly connected. Everything is end-to-end encrypted. No accounts, no servers, no history. Gone when you close the tab.

Frequently asked questions

Is Btwinus free?

Yes. There is no subscription, no ads, and no premium tier. Everything runs in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Do I need to install an app or create an account?

No. Open the link in any modern browser. No email, no phone number, no sign-up of any kind.

Where are my messages stored?

Nowhere. Messages travel directly between the two browsers over an encrypted WebRTC connection. When either person closes the tab, the conversation is gone.

Why do I have to send the passphrase separately?

The link is encrypted with the passphrase. If both travel through the same channel, whoever can read that channel has both. Sending the passphrase by phone, SMS, or in person means an attacker would have to compromise two channels instead of one.

How is this different from Signal or WhatsApp?

Those need an app, a phone number, and a company’s servers to route messages. Btwinus needs none of that. It is built for one-off private conversations, not as a replacement for your everyday messenger.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge on iOS and Android. The invite link can be shown as a QR code so the other person only has to scan it.
